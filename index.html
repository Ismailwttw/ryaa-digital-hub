Our Departments & Activities

Working across multiple fronts for community development and advocacy.

Refugee Affairs

Focuses on protection rights, legal aid awareness, and advocacy for refugee welfare.

Foreign Affairs

Builds international partnerships and diplomatic engagement with global human rights bodies.

Education

Promotes access to quality learning, skill development workshops, and youth scholarships.

Youth Empowerment

Empowers young leaders through training, mentorship, and civic participation programs.

Media

Manages public relations, digital storytelling, press releases, and social media campaigns.

Culture

Preserves and promotes Rohingya heritage, arts, literature, and traditional history.

Environment

Leads climate awareness, tree plantation drives, and sustainable camp management initiatives.

Community Engagement

Fosters social cohesion, grassroots dialogues, and community support networks.
